# Logo

The primary Manazashi logo combines the wordmark with an abstract indexed page.
A fine line passes through the page like a sight line. The first `a` has a
circular pupil, while the second `a` winks.

![Manazashi logo](assets/manazashi-logo.svg)

## Files

- [`manazashi-logo.svg`](assets/manazashi-logo.svg) is the primary logo.
- [`manazashi-logo-eyes.svg`](assets/manazashi-logo-eyes.svg) uses explicit eye
  shapes in both `a` letterforms.
- [`manazashi-logo-wordmark-eyes.svg`](assets/manazashi-logo-wordmark-eyes.svg)
  is the wordmark-only variant with explicit eyes.

All variants have a transparent background and use vector paths rather than a
font, so their letterforms do not depend on the rendering environment. The
artwork uses `#182226` for its strokes and fills.

## Usage

Use the primary logo where horizontal space permits. Preserve its aspect ratio,
leave clear space around the artwork, and avoid adding a background or effects
that obscure the thin sight lines. The alternate variants are exploratory
companions rather than replacements for the primary logo.

Markdown:

```markdown
![manazashi](docs/assets/manazashi-logo.svg)
```

When referencing the logo from a document under `docs/`, use
`assets/manazashi-logo.svg` instead.
